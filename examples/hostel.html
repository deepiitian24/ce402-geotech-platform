Inputs and assumptions

The 30 × 15 m grid has 5 m bays. Service column loads use λ = 1.06. C4 has no specified load row. For a reproducible sensitivity baseline only, assume unscaled P = 1450 kN and Mᵧ = 150 kN·m, giving 1537 kN and 159 kN·m. Confirm both and the moment axis before adopting a design. All footings start at 1.5 m bgl. Gross V adds 0.5 m of concrete at 24 kN/m³ and 1.0 m of backfill at 18 kN/m³, over the full footing area. Water loading is represented consistently by total service loads and excavation relief. No horizontal load was supplied.

Drained shear regressions (c′ in kPa, φ′ in degrees) for SM unsoaked, SM soaked and SP soaked respectively give 6.9 / 31.0, 1.0 / 30.0, 0.4 / 34.4. Use soaked angles for seasonal groundwater and neglect apparent cohesion. Transfer BH-1 sand angles to the other holes as an assumption. The two UU series give mean sᵤ of 28.5 and 36.1 kPa from half the failure deviator; these describe BH-1 clay. Exclude the cobble strike at 9 m and retain refusal as a penetration observation. Neither provides a representative numerical clay or gravel N.

For UDS-1, 1 kgf/cm² = 98.0665 kPa. The virgin 2–8 kgf/cm² slope gives Cc = 0.292 and the 8–0.5 unloading slope gives Cr = 0.038. Adopt the loading knee near 1 kgf/cm² as an approximate σ′p of 98 kPa. The 2 kgf/cm² increment gives cᵥ = 6.3 × 10⁻⁸ m²/s. BH-1 uses e₀ = 0.918. Assume saturated clay and Gs = 2.70 at the other holes, giving e₀ = wGs. Transfer BH-1 compression parameters provisionally and vary both slopes at BH-3. Drainage is assumed double between permeable granular strata. Granular moduli (SM/SP, MPa) are assumed 20/42, 18/35 and 9/22 west to east based on relative SPT resistance; gravel modulus is assumed 80 MPa. These are stiffness estimates, not laboratory measurements.

Method and sensitivity

Granular settlement uses Schmertmann et al. (1978), at one year for its creep factor. Clay settlement is ultimate primary settlement on sublayers ≤0.5 m, summing signed Newmark rectangular corner stresses from all footings at each midpoint. For C4, use a uniform effective rectangle centred under the resultant. Local granular settlement omits adjacent-footing interaction. The calculation assumes laterally continuous layers within each borehole zone. Actual transition geometry needs confirmation.

Foundation decision

Reject this shallow trial. Wider bases within 5 m bays have limited clearance and still load a continuous compressible clay layer. A raft can redistribute column loads, but also loads the full clay thickness. Prefer investigation of a single piled foundation system through the clay into the very dense gravel, including the zone transitions, to reduce differential movement. The top of gravel is at 9.6, 10.6 and 11.2 m bgl west to east. A conceptual four-pile cap beneath an ordinary interior column carries about 384 kN per pile from the 1537 kN column load alone, before cap weight and moment redistribution. This is demand only. Select diameter, penetration and spacing from deeper investigation and load testing; verify group settlement, downdrag, seismic/lateral response, boulders and stream-side groundwater before accepting the alternative.
