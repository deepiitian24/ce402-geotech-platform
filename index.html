Equation sources and IS codes
  • Effective stress: Terzaghi's principle, σ′v0 = σv0 − u, with γmoist above and γsat below the water table, u = γw(z − zw).
  • SPT overburden correction: IS 2131:1981 Cl. 3.6.1 and Fig. 1 (Peck-Hanson-Thornburn), CN = 0.77 log₁₀(2000/σ′v0), limited to 0.40 ≤ CN ≤ 2.0; N′ = CN·N.
  • SPT dilatancy correction: IS 2131:1981 Cl. 3.6.2, N″ = 15 + 0.5(N′ − 15) for fine sand or silt below the water table with N′ > 15.
  • φ′ from SPT: Meyerhof (1956), Peck et al. (1974); approximate check only.
  • Direct shear: ordinary least squares fit of τ = c′ + σn tan φ′; Lambe & Whitman (1969); test procedure IS 2720 (Part 13):1986.
  • Bearing capacity: IS 6403:1981 Cl. 5.1.2, qnu = c Nc sc dc ic + q(Nq − 1) sq dq iq + 0.5 γ B′ Nγ sγ dγ iγ W′; qns = qnu/FoS; qs = qns + q. Water table factor W′ per Cl. 5.1.2.2; local shear cm = ⅔c′, tan φm = ⅔ tan φ′.
  • Vertical stress under a rectangle: Newmark (1935) corner solution; Das (2010) Principles of Foundation Engineering, Eq. 10.15. Centre = 4 × corner of B/2 × L/2.
  • Circular and strip: Boussinesq (1885); Das (2010) Eq. 10.19; Poulos & Davis (1974).
  • 2:1 method: Terzaghi & Peck (1967); Bowles (1996), Δσz = qBL/((B+z)(L+z)).
  • Granular settlement: Schmertmann et al. (1978), S = C₁C₂ Δq Σ(Iz/Es)Δz, C₁ = 1 − 0.5(σ′v0/Δq) ≥ 0.5, C₂ = 1 + 0.2 log₁₀(t/0.1).
  • Clay consolidation: S = C/(1+e₀) · H · log₁₀(σ′f/σ′v0) with Cc (NC) or Cr (OC), split at σ′p when crossing; sublayers ≤ 0.5 m.
  • Time rate: Terzaghi 1D theory, Tv = cvt/Hdr²; U = √(4Tv/π) for U ≤ 60 %, otherwise Tv = 1.781 − 0.933 log₁₀(100 − U%). Hdr = H (single) or H/2 (double).
